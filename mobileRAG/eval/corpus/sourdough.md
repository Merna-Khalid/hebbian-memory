# Sourdough Starter Care

Feed the starter twice daily at a 1:1:1 ratio (starter, flour, water) when kept at room temperature. Use unbleached bread flour; rye flour speeds up fermentation. The starter is ready to bake when it doubles in 4–6 hours.

For long storage, dry the starter on parchment and keep it in the freezer. Revive with two days of twice-daily feedings.

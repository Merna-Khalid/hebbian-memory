# Gym Program

Push day is Monday and Thursday: bench press 4×8 at 60 kg, overhead press 3×10, incline dumbbell press 3×12. Pull day is Tuesday and Friday: barbell rows 4×8, lat pulldowns 3×10, face pulls 3×15.

Legs on Saturday: squats 4×6 at 80 kg, Romanian deadlifts 3×8, calf raises. Sunday is rest or an easy 5k run along the corniche.

Deload every sixth week. Creatine daily, 5 g with breakfast; protein target 140 g per day.
